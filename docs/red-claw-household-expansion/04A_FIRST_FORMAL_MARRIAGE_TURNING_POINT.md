## 1. Canon profile preserved

Dagmar is the first of Christian Red IV's exactly three official wives. She is 37 at the current campaign date and is identified as First Wife and Commander of the Frontier and Border Guard. Her compiled Canon profile describes her as tall and broad-shouldered, with long auburn hair usually in a duty braid, hazel eyes and a scar through the left eyebrow. Her voice is low, level and dry; she uses short sentences, rarely asks a question she cannot answer, and becomes quieter and more formal when angry.

Her established priorities are a trusted gate roster and a succession that does not require her children to bleed for it. She fears losing a child to war or a gate failing on her watch. Her Canon day includes a dawn wall walk, Gate Council mornings, midday drills, evenings with Liese and the Hall at night. She responds to competence and clear reports, and resists flattery or theatrical worry. These details are preserved from the CP9 compiled card; the expansion does not replace or dilute them.

Dagmar's family branch is listed as the Karl Line / paternal branch in the proposed registry based on the supplied family-branch label. Her precise parents and the full blood-degree route are not certified here. Floor 7's Family Ledger must approve any new in-law or succession edges.

## 3. Why Dagmar

The existing Canon identity is retained. Her established military command, Red Claw family standing and clear-eyed attitude to succession make her a credible spouse in a household seeking durable administration and a disciplined public line. This is a compatibility reading of the established profile, not proof that her family negotiated a particular contract.

Dagmar is not an ornamental solution to Christian's reputation. She has her own authority, agenda and non-negotiable interests: the security of the frontier, the competence of her roster, and a succession that does not consume her children. Any marriage arrangement must be compatible with her own agency and with the existing legal/customary framework.

1. Christian III raises a dynastic concern privately with his adult son. The simulation must not write Christian IV's response, agreement or emotions; those remain player-controlled historical decisions already fixed only if the user explicitly approves this backstory.
2. The father or household representatives open formal discussions with Dagmar's branch. Whether Dagmar was approached first, what she negotiated and which family officers participated are UNKNOWN until approved.
3. Dagmar evaluates the practical consequences: security authority, her own position, children and succession expectations. Her established personality favours direct information rather than flattery.
4. The marriage becomes formal only after an in-world legal/ceremonial record exists. A family rumour, proposal or engagement is not a marriage.
5. The first marriage becomes a turning point in household expectations: public conduct, staff protocol, family access and succession administration become more formal. Prior friendships are not automatically severed, and prior partners do not automatically become hostile.
