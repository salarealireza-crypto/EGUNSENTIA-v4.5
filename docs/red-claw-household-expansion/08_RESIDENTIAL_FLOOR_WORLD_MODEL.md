# Red Claw Residential-Floor World Model

**Status:** functional worldbuilding specification; proposed areas remain conceptual until checked against authoritative room/atlas/map data. This is not a confirmed architectural floor plan.

## 1. Confirmed geography anchors

- **Floor 6 — the Dome:** current residence anchor. CP9 identifies the Dome as the household environment, but does not provide a complete room-by-room population plan.
- **Floor 7 — Family Ledger / Kin Hall beside the Relic Cabinet:** established location for genealogy and kinship records. It remains the single genealogical source of truth; this update does not create a parallel bloodline ledger.
- Existing named settings such as the Dome, training/gallery spaces, reception/social spaces and guard spaces remain as authored in CP9. No new adjacency or exact coordinates are claimed.

## 3. Access model

Access is a role + place + time permission, not simply relationship status. Family membership does not create permission to enter every chamber. A guest invitation does not grant access to medical records, sealed correspondence or the Floor 7 genealogy ledger. Household staff do not receive military-command authority; Iron Guard patrols do not become domestic chores. Children use supervised learning and communal spaces and are excluded from adult private relationship systems.

## 4. Daily society

The intended recurring rhythm is morning health/attendance check, breakfast and roster handover; education and work blocks; lunch and family service; afternoon administration, visits, maintenance and training; evening meal, family discussion and social gatherings; night guard handover and quiet hours. Individual CAST routines may describe character availability, but recurring Planner/Calendar events are not yet generated from them.

## 5. Population model

The 100-card registry includes non-residents, departed members, deceased people and duty-based presences. It is not a building occupancy count. The proposed current local count of 71 permanent family-linked residents includes Christian IV as a player household resident; CAST excludes player-controlled Christian and includes two departed Grotsen relatives, giving 72 active CAST records. Neither number is the total residential-floor population. A complete census needs servants, medical staff, security, temporary guests, visitors, dependants and absent-person dates, none of which can be derived from CP9 with confidence.
