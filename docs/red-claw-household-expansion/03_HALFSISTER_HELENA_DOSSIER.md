## Identity and appearance

Helena resembles Mirelda in dark hair, attentive eyes and controlled posture, but has a small pale birthmark near the left ear. Her voice is clear and mid-range. She often asks a direct question, listens to the answer, and only then offers her own view. No height or exact measurements are assigned.

## Temperament and personal direction

Independent, curious about both halves of her family, and slow to trust factional narratives. She keeps a small collection of repaired objects from the Dome and sketches doors and stairwells from memory. These objects are not proof of a secret plot; they give her a private way to preserve a childhood place she left.

Her ambition is to build a profession and social standing that do not depend on Christian IV or Mirelda's marriage. Her fear is being treated as a diplomatic bridge, or being asked to choose a family side in public. Proposed education includes Grotsen literacy, history, bookkeeping and administration, followed by self-study of Red Claw military history.
