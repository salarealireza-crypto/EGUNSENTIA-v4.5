# Historical Red Claw Household — Father's Generation

**Status:** proposed historical reconstruction; not CP9 Canon.  
**Working period:** approximately 76–98 AW, subject to date validation.

## 1. Population and time frame

The requested historical adult core is exactly ten people: Christian Red III, Mirelda Grotsen, three male domestic servants and five female Iron Guard members. Christian Red IV is an additional child/dependant. Under the proposed birth window for Helena Red-Grotsen, she joins the household snapshot from 80 AW onward.

## 2. Household administration and authority

Christian Red III is the proposed final domestic authority. Mirelda is a senior family member through marriage and motherhood, but she does not automatically control the Guard. Arno Bittner serves as proposed senior household steward and manages rosters, ledger, maintenance and domestic administration. Ragna Stahl commands the five-woman Iron Guard detail. Markus Fenn controls stores and meal-service records; Pavel Kessler handles authorised correspondence and guest records. Guards retain professional security authority, not domestic-labour authority.

## 3. Mirelda's Grotsen position

Mirelda's House Grotsen origin and stepmother status are Canon. Her exact cadet branch, parents, date of marriage, factional mandate and political motives are unknown. This reconstruction proposes that her marriage makes her a visible kinship bridge between Grotsen and Red Claw households, but leaves whether the marriage was arranged, affectionate, pragmatic or politically imposed unresolved. She is not a generic antagonist: her core proposed conflict is the protection of her daughter's independent standing while refusing both factions' attempts to simplify her past.

## 5. Architecture and routines

The historical plan is a functional zoning proposal, not a confirmed floor map. It includes a reception vestibule, family apartments, steward office, servants' quarters, Guard post, kitchen/pantry/dining room, children's study, first-aid/recovery room, and stores/service passage. No exact floor number, dimensions, room adjacency or current Floor 6 equivalence is claimed.

A typical proposed day begins with the steward's ledger and the Guard's shift handover. Stores are checked before breakfast; household and children's routines follow; visitors are registered and escorted; family dining is distinct from staff meal issue; the evening concludes with a Guard handover and a final domestic roster check. These are role templates, not events already scheduled in the game calendar.

## 6. Father's death and household transition

Christian III's death date is proposed as 19 Ironmonth 98 AW solely to provide a working chronology, not to assert a canon fact. Mirelda and Helena's departure to the Grotsen Dominion is also a proposal based on the requested family outcome. Do not automatically dismiss, reassign, kill or transfer Arno, Markus, Pavel, Ragna, Vera, Erika, Marta or Selma. Their later service and survival records are unknown until an event is approved. Christian IV's succession and residence are governed by existing Red Claw law; this file creates no new title or inheritance right.
