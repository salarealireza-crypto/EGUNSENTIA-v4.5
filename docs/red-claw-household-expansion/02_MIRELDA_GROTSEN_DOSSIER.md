# Mirelda Grotsen — Stepmother Dossier

**Identity status:** Canon-confirmed (Christian IV's stepmother; House Grotsen origin).  
**Biography status:** proposed.  
**Candidate DOB:** 8 First Light 44 AW (proposed only; age 61 at the 105 AW campaign date if accepted).  
**Current residence:** Grotsen Dominion; exact home unknown.  
**Family links:** wife of Christian Red III (date/status needs date validation); mother of proposed Helena Red-Grotsen; stepmother, not biological mother, of Christian Red IV.

## Character concept

Mirelda is composed, socially observant and strategically cautious without being passive. She knows how to occupy a room without dominating it. Her silence is deliberate: she usually waits until everyone has committed to a position before asking one precise question. Her central motivation is to protect her daughter's right to exist as a person in both family histories, not as a bargaining token.

## Appearance and manner

Proposed appearance: spare, upright posture; dark hair worn in a practical low knot with a pale streak at the right temple; composed face and watchful eyes. No height, body measurements or arbitrary anatomical details are assigned. Her movement is measured; she straightens nearby objects when thinking. Her voice is a quiet alto, careful with names and titles. A deliberate pause is more characteristic than raising her voice.

Her presentation favours restrained Grotsen tailoring and functional layers rather than conspicuous ornament. Specific colours, insignia and ceremonial dress remain open until the Grotsen visual canon is consulted.

## Origin and genealogy

Canon establishes only her House Grotsen origin. The proposed profile places her in a lesser Grotsen cadet branch, but parents, siblings, branch name and precise rank remain UNKNOWN. Her marriage into Red Claw creates marriage-kin status; it does not make her a biological Red. Floor 7's Family Ledger must be checked before fixing her exact kinship connections or political inheritance.

## Marriage and household position

Proposed: Mirelda becomes Christian III's wife after Katharina's death. The source does not prove whether the match was arranged, negotiated as an alliance, freely chosen, or a combination. The profile therefore avoids a definitive motive. Her place in the household depends on marriage, personal judgement and her role in raising Christian IV; she is not automatically steward, commander or faction ruler.

A useful dramatic tension is that Red Claw may value the Grotsen connection while Grotsen may value her access to Red Claw. Mirelda resists being reduced to either side's messenger. Her strongest political tool is her ability to compare both families' versions of an event and remember what each leaves out.

## Relationship with Christian IV

Canon says she helped raise Christian after Katharina's death. The exact emotional quality is not established. Proposed interpretation: affectionate and protective, but occasionally strained by her divided loyalties and her insistence on household rules. She does not claim to replace Katharina. As Christian grows older, she respects his authority while remaining willing to challenge his judgement privately.

## Relationship with Helena

Mirelda is protective but not smothering. She wants Helena to know the Red Claw side of her parentage without being compelled to perform loyalty to it. She would correct false dates and family rumours, but would not disclose private correspondence to prove a point. Her worst fear is that either house will frame Helena as a diplomatic asset.

## Habits, routine, ambitions and fears

- Keeps two appointment books: one for formal obligations, one for personal promises.
- Straightens an object when thinking through a difficult choice.
- Proposed routine: morning correspondence and accounts; midday family/ward visits; evening reading and private letters.
- Ambition: preserve Helena's independent place in Grotsen society while preventing her Red Claw years from being rewritten as faction propaganda.
- Fear: that a household will treat Helena as a political token, or erase the complexity of Mirelda's marriage and years in the Dome.

## Knowledge boundaries

Mirelda knows her own marriage and daughter’s parentage. She does not automatically know Christian's private decisions, Elara's black-layer operations, sealed investigations or current relationship state. Her statements must be grounded in letters, witnessed events or direct conversation.

2. A visit to the Dome tests whether old household courtesies survive.
3. Helena asks for a meeting with Christian without factional witnesses.
4. An old appointment book exposes a promise made by Christian III, but not necessarily whether it was fulfilled.
